# Frame

The user cannot inspect the eight scoring criteria in Settings and wants to edit
their descriptions and relative weights. They explicitly selected the existing
eight fixed criteria, then approved [design.md](design.md), including descriptions
up to 1000 characters, integer weights 1–10 and historical snapshots.

Success means that a saved owner-scoped rubric affects both the next eligible
model request and its deterministic score. Completed and interrupted reserved
analyses retain their original rules. Source evidence and budget safeguards stay
mandatory. Changing the criterion set, model, paid retry policy or partial-result
policy is outside this change.
