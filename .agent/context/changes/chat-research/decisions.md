# Decisions

Reuse the existing provider, fetcher, contracts and ledger. Add two call slots
rather than reuse paid reservations. Stop research after an uncertain response.
Keep all newly fetched sources across the two iterations. Normalize whitespace
and case for duplicate-query prevention; semantic equivalence is not guaranteed.

to one; the updated cost disclosure explicitly sends two. No server inference of
expanded consent. Extend only new turn deadlines to eight minutes for the bounded
three-generation/two-fetch-batch path. No extra evaluator, fallback or dependency.
